public function regex_replace(re, text, replacement, replace_all) result(out_text)

The M_unicode__regex(3) module loaded into the M_unicode(3) module compiles regular expression patterns into Nondeterministic Finite Automaton (NFA) bytecode and executes them over arrays of UTF-32 scalar codepoints (integer(int32)). Because matching operates directly on Unicode codepoints rather than raw UTF-8 bytes, multi-byte characters (accented Latin, CJK, emoji, symbols) are indexed and matched as single characters without splitting byte sequences.
